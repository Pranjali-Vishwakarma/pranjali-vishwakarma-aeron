Question 2 · Where should the technician go next week?

Send them to S04 Foothills Community Garden.

Its thermometer reads too warm in sunshine, and the error grows every week: daily highs went from about 1 °C too warm in late March to about 3–4 °C by mid-April. This cannot be fixed afterwards in the data. Only a visit stops it.

  1. First, before touching anything: in sunshine, hold a handheld thermometer in shade beside the shield for 5 minutes; note both readings. This confirms the cause.
  2. Then the shield (white stack of plates around the sensor): the 15 March repaint of the post may have put paint in its vents. Clean the vents or fit a spare shield.
  3. Rain gauge: check the funnel is still clear after the 2 April clean-out and fit a leaf guard.

Why not S03? Its thermometer is failing too (it froze for three days and sometimes sends impossible values), but in between it reads correctly and the bad values are easy to remove. A volunteer can swap in a spare probe. S04's error hides inside normal-looking numbers, so it does more harm.

Question 1 · Which data is good enough for the spring summary?

Nearly all of it. Leave out the red parts below.

We found 11 real faults. Two cannot be repaired (red below). We have already corrected or removed the rest. No station needs to be dropped.

Do not use for the summary:

  • S04 daily maximum temperatures from late March onwards (night-time lows are fine).
  • S04 day-by-day rain, 23 March to 2 April. The blocked gauge released all the stored water at once when cleaned, so its six-week total is still right (33 mm, neighbours 28–45 mm).

Use with care: S07 wind after 26 March. The new anemometer reads about a third of the true speed. We scaled it up (roughly ×3.5–4), but that is an estimate: fine for "windy or calm", not for wind records.

S01 Saddle Ridge Hut sits 1,000 m above the rest. It is colder, windier and lower in pressure for real, so report it separately from the network average. Most of its "rain" was snow.

Small jobs that don't need the technician

  • S06Ask the fire station to set the logger clock to UTC, not local time. Every new reading is still 6 hours off.
  • S07Ask the farm to photograph the new anemometer's label. The model number gives the exact factor to replace our estimate.
  • S03Order and post a spare temperature probe to the library.
  • S08Put the SIM on automatic top-up. S02Calibrate the new pressure board when the kit is free.
